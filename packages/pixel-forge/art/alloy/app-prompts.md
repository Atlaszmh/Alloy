# Gemini app prompts

For making sprites in the Gemini app with your subscription instead of the API.
Written by `forge prompts`; run it again after changing `style.json` or `manifest.json`.

1. Start a new chat for each sprite and attach `reference.png` (next to this file) so it matches the existing art.
2. Paste the prompt. Regenerate until you have two or three you like.
3. Download each image as PNG and name it after the sprite: `frost_wolf.png`, `frost_wolf-2.png`, and so on.
4. Put them in `inbox/` next to this file (on GitHub: open the folder, then Add file → Upload files) and run `forge import`.

## frost_wolf (16×16)

```text
A single 16x16 pixel art sprite of a lean white frost wolf with pale blue fur, icicle hackles and glowing icy blue eyes, for a high-fantasy action RPG. Top-down three-quarter view: the camera looks down at it from the front, the creature faces the viewer. Very low resolution with big chunky square pixels: the whole sprite is drawn on a 16 by 16 grid. Flat colors only. No gradients, no anti-aliasing, no dithering, no blur. At most 10 colors. A 1-pixel near-black outline around the silhouette. Light comes from the top-left. Centered, feet or base at the bottom, filling most of the frame. Bold readable silhouette. Plain solid magenta (#FF00FF) background. No shadow, no ground, no scenery, no text, no border, no frame. The attached image shows sprites from the same game. Match their pixel art style, palette, outline weight and scale, but draw only the new creature, alone.
```
